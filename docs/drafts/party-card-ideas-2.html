Party card: 4 more ideas

More Skyline, the team compared against each other, and the "That's … stopped as a team" line in every one. Same sizes as now. Each theme would get its own spin (written under each idea).

D · Skyline race

Each person is a building. Taller = more kg, lit windows = sets done. The tallest gets a gold outline and a crown. You can see who carried the team in one glance. Summit: three peaks with flags. Boulder: climbing ropes at different heights on the chalk wall. Agent: three sniper-scope rings that fill up.

E · Head to head

For a party of 2, or you against your best rival in a bigger party. Lift by lift, each person's best set, with a star for whoever won it, plus a lift score like "3–2". Only lifts you both did show up. Summit: "Summit duel". Boulder: chalked tally marks. Agent: a "Mission debrief" with redacted bars.

F · Comic page

A comic page with one panel per person. Each panel has a speech bubble for what they did best ("Most sets!", "2 PRs!", "Done first!"), their share of the team total as a bar, and an MVP sticker on the top lifter. The taxi line is the big panel at the bottom. Summit: trail-map panels. Boulder: chalk-tag panels. Agent: dossier photos with stamps.

G · Front page

A city newspaper front page. The headline is the taxi line, there's a "photo" column for each person with their numbers, and a small results table. It looks totally different from a normal workout card, so it stands out in a feed. Summit: "Mountain Post" trail bulletin. Boulder: a gym noticeboard flyer. Agent: a classified file with stamps.
