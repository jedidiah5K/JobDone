Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

The short version

  • JobDone stores what you log so you can see it on any device you sign in on.
  • Your data is only visible to your own account.
  • No ads, no analytics trackers, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
  • You can export everything or delete your account in the app at any time.

Who runs JobDone

JobDone is a small personal project, currently in a soft launch with friends. It is not a company. Questions or requests go through Me → Send feedback in the app.

What we store

  • Account: your email address, your first name if you give one, and a password (stored as a secure hash by our login provider, never in plain text).
  • What you log: workouts, sets, splits, exercises and notes, body weight, steps, machine setup notes, and anything else you type into the app.
  • Optional extras: body measurements and progress photos, only if you choose to add them. Photos are shrunk before upload.
  • Feedback you send from the app, with your email so we can reply.
  • Step sync: if you set up the Apple Shortcut, it sends daily step totals to JobDone using a sync key. Only a scrambled (hashed) copy of that key is stored.

We don't collect your location, contacts or health records beyond what you choose to enter or send. JobDone doesn't use cookies for tracking; the app keeps a copy of your data on your device so it works without signal.

Why we store it

Only to run the app for you: save your log, show your progress, sync between your devices, and fix problems you report.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase (database and login, servers in Singapore). The website is served by Vercel. Both process data only to provide those services. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and database rules stop any account from reading another account's data.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Deleting a workout removes it; deleting your account (Me → Delete account) permanently removes your account and everything in it straight away. Copies may remain in our providers' routine backups for a short time before they expire.

Your choices

  • See and export: Me → Your data → Export backup (JSON) or spreadsheet (CSV).
  • Correct: edit or delete anything you've logged in the app.
  • Delete: Me → Delete account.
  • Ask a question: Me → Send feedback.

Age

JobDone is for people aged 13 and over. If you're under 13, please don't create an account. If we learn that a child under 13 has signed up, we'll delete the account.

Health information

JobDone is a training log, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if you have an injury or health condition.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before it applies.
