Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

The short version

  • Job’s Done stores what you log so you can see it on any device you sign in on.
  • Your log is private. What you choose to share goes only to friends you accept, and a little of it (your first name, your comments) can be seen by their friends too.
  • No ads, no analytics trackers, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
  • You can export everything or delete your account in the app at any time.

Who runs Job’s Done

Job’s Done is run by its developer, an independent developer in the Philippines. It isn’t a company, and it’s in an early beta. The developer decides how your data is used and is also the data protection officer. For privacy questions, requests or complaints, use Me → Send feedback in the app or email chuajedidiahobrent@gmail.com.

What we store

  • Account: your email address, your first name if you give one, and a password (stored as a secure hash by our login provider, never in plain text).
  • What you log: workouts, sets, splits, exercises and notes, body weight, steps, machine setup notes, and anything else you type into the app.
  • Optional extras: body measurements and progress photos, only if you choose to add them. Photos are shrunk before upload.
  • Feedback you send from the app, with your email so we can reply.
  • Step sync: if you set up the Apple Shortcut, it sends daily step totals to Job’s Done using a sync key. Only a scrambled (hashed) copy of that key is stored.
  • Error notes: if the app hits an error while you’re signed in, it saves a short note (the error message, where in the app it happened, the app version and your browser type) so it can be fixed. These are deleted after 30 days.
  • Two-step sign-in: if you turn it on, our login provider keeps the secret your authenticator app uses to make codes.
  • Notifications: if you turn on rest alerts, we keep the push address your phone gives us (it lets Apple or Google deliver the notification). Turning them off removes the push address; deleting your account removes it too.
  • ID card and friends: when you open your ID card, we store what’s on it (your first name and theme, plus whichever of these you leave switched on: level, time training, workout count, the lifts you pick, your split, home gym, streak, favourite lift, total kg lifted, PR count, workouts this month, badges, a one-line motto you write, and body weight and height, which start off), a friend code, and a photo if you add one. The photo is shrunk on your phone to a small picture before it’s sent, and you can remove it any time. Lifts you type in by hand show without the ✓ that logged lifts get. Someone sends a request with your code and they become a friend once you accept. Removing a friend takes you off each other’s list; deleting your account removes your card and friends.
  • Friends activity: if you leave “Share my training” on, friends see whether you trained or marked a rest day today, the split you did, and how many workouts you’ve done this week. When you finish a workout, a Done Stamp (split, volume, time, number of lifts and PRs) goes to friends, with a photo only if you add one. Stories are photos you choose to post, with an optional line of text. Friends can react, comment and send you a once-a-day nudge, which you can turn off. Stamps and stories show for 24 hours and are deleted within 48.
  • Comments and reactions you leave on friends’ posts. They’re deleted with the post.
  • At the gym: if you tap “Tell friends I’m at the gym”, your friends see the name of your gym until you stop or for up to 3 hours.
  • Blocks: if you block someone, we keep a note of who you blocked so they can’t send you requests.
  • Reports: if you report a post or a comment, we keep a copy of its text (not the photo), who posted it and who reported it, so we can look at it. When two people report the same thing, it’s hidden until we do. An account that breaks the rules can be stopped from posting for a while.
  • Sign-up check: to stop bots, we keep a scrambled copy of the network address you signed up from.
  • Limits: we count how many friend codes you try, so nobody can guess codes.
  • My Gym (beta): if you turn it on, we store which gyms you joined, the machines you added and your “it’s here” / “not here” answers. Other members of that gym see the machines and how many people confirmed each one, never your name or email. Leaving a gym or deleting your account removes your membership and answers. Machines you added stay on that gym’s list without your name.

We don’t collect your contacts or your phone’s location. The only location we keep is the gym you choose to show your friends.

Why we’re allowed to

  • To run the app you signed up for: your account, your log, syncing between devices, friends and posts. Without this the app can’t work.
  • Your consent, for body and health data: body weight, height, measurements and progress photos count as health information. We store them only because you chose to enter them. You can take that back by deleting them or your account.
  • Keeping the service safe: sign-up checks, limits, error notes, blocks and reports. We need these to stop abuse and fix problems.

We never use your data for ads, never sell it, and never use it to train AI.

Where it lives and who handles it

Your data is stored with Supabase (database and login) on servers in Singapore. The website is served by Vercel, a US company, through servers around the world. If you turn on notifications, Apple or Google deliver them to your phone. These companies only handle data to provide their service to us.

This means your data leaves the country you live in. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and database rules stop any account from reading another account’s data.

Who can see what

  • Only you: your workouts, sets, notes, body weight, measurements, progress photos, steps and everything on Today and Progress.
  • Friends you accept: your ID card (only the parts you leave on), whether you trained today, your Done Stamps and stories, and the gym you’re at if you choose to show it.
  • Friends of your friends: your first name and what you wrote, when you comment on a post they can also see.
  • Anyone with your friend code: your first name, when they send you a request. They see nothing else unless you accept.

Friends can save or share a photo you post. Once they have, deleting the post doesn’t remove their copy.

How long we keep it

  • Your log, ID card and settings: until you delete them or your account.
  • Done Stamps, stories, comments and reactions: shown for 24 hours, deleted within 48.
  • Error notes: 30 days.
  • Feedback and reports you send: 12 months, or until you delete your account if that comes first.
  • Daily safety copies of your data: 14 days. They may still hold things you deleted in that time.

Deleting your account (Me → Delete account) removes your account and everything above straight away, safety copies included. Our providers may keep their own encrypted backups for a short time before those expire.

On your phone

The app keeps a few things on your phone so it opens fast and works without signal: your sign-in, a copy of your log, which screen you were on, posts you hid, and your gym list. They’re needed for the app to work, so there’s no cookie banner. Nothing on your phone is used to track you. Signing out removes your sign-in. On a shared phone, sign out when you’re done.

Your rights

  • See and export your data: Me → Your data → Export backup (JSON) or spreadsheet (CSV).
  • Correct it: edit or delete anything you’ve logged.
  • Delete it: Me → Delete account.
  • Take back consent for body and health data: delete those entries, or your account.
  • Object or ask questions about how we use your data: Me → Send feedback, or chuajedidiahobrent@gmail.com.
  • Complain to a privacy regulator. In the Philippines that’s the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph). In the EU or UK it’s your local data protection authority. Please tell us first if you can, so we can try to fix it.

If something goes wrong

If your data is ever exposed by mistake or by an attack, we’ll tell the people affected and report it to the National Privacy Commission when the law requires, within 72 hours of finding out. We’ll say what happened, what data was involved and what you can do.

Age

Job’s Done is for people aged 13 and over. If you’re under 13, please don’t create an account. If we learn that a child under 13 has signed up, we’ll delete the account.

Health information

Job’s Done is a training log, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if you have an injury or health condition.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we’ll say so in the app before it applies.
